AstroGrass Database: Studies and Candidate Genes

A catalogue of grass spaceflight and related omics studies, and 29 gravitropism candidate genes defined by Arabidopsis anchor genes, with their Brachypodium, rice and wheat orthologs from Ensembl Compara. Spaceflight values are Su et al.'s (2023, Supplemental Tables S2–S4, CC BY 4.0) and appear only where Su et al. list a gene as a DEG; no new analysis of OSD-375 has been run here.
