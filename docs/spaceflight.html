Ecotype-Specific Response Magnitudes

Empirical RNA-Seq profiling from the ISS Veggie facility (Su et al. 2023, Life 13:633) revealed pronounced accession-specific response diversity:

  • Bd21 (Reference Line): 1,027 differentially expressed genes in shoots and 325 in roots.
  • Bd21-3 (Transformable Standard): Muted response with fewer DEGs, reflecting genetic divergence from standard Bd21.
  • Gaz8 (Turkish Accession): Most divergent transcriptome trajectory, with pronounced activation of calcium signaling kinases (CPK28, CAS) and cell wall remodeling machinery.
