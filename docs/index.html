Brachypodium GWAS-Spaceflight Integration

Connecting gravitropic natural variation with ISS microgravity responses

The Story

This project connects gravitropism analyzed via Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS) with transcriptomic responses to spaceflight from the NASA OSDR OSD-375 (APEX-06) mission. By integrating these distinct layers, we aim to uncover the underlying genetic architecture of gravitropic responses in the model grass Brachypodium distachyon and draw cross-species comparisons, offering critical implications for Bioregenerative Life Support Systems (BLSS).

Key Findings

GWAS Targets

Identified novel loci associated with gravitropic reorientation.

Spaceflight Transcriptomics

Differentially expressed genes in root and shoot across three ecotypes.

Integrative Model

Convergence of natural variation and microgravity responses.
