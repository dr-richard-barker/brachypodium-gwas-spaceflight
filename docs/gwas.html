🧬 Gravitropic Kinetics & GWAS Results

Genome-wide association mapping and natural variation in gravitropic reorientation kinetics across Mediterranean diversity accessions of the model cereal grass Brachypodium distachyon.

Gravitropic Reorientation Kinetics & Genome-Wide Association Study

Figure 1. (A) Time-course gravitropic reorientation kinetics across 15 accessions following 90° gravistimulation, highlighting OSD-375 spaceflight accessions Bd21, Bd21-3, and Gaz8.
