🧭 Plans and Withdrawn Analyses

How the AstroGrass resource is put together, what is planned next, and which earlier results were withdrawn.

AstroGrass Resource

Figure 2. AstroGrass catalogues five studies, OSD-375 (Brachypodium, ISS), OSD-622 (dwarf wheat, ISS), GSE97940 and GSE48040 (Brachypodium ground RNA-seq) and PXD000868 (Brachypodium leaf phosphoproteome), alongside the gravitropism candidate-gene table. The catalogue and table are served as CSV and JSON and searched in the AstroGrass database.

Planned Analyses (Not Yet Run)

  1. Brachypodium orthologs. Correct the 12 Arabidopsis anchors that name the wrong gene, then map Brachypodium orthologs of all 29 candidates with Ensembl Compara, recording the release and any one-to-many cases.
  2. Spaceflight expression of the candidates. Take the per-accession DEG tables published by Su et al. (2023, supplementary tables, CC BY 4.0) and report the candidates' values, credited to that study.
  3. New differential expression, if needed. Start from the OSD-375 FASTQ files and account for the confounding of accession with sequencing batch.
  4. Gravitropism measurements. Add numeric measurements for the accession panel, with their source documented.
  5. Association with genotype. Needs genotype data for the phenotyped accessions; with tens of accessions it would be a candidate-gene comparison rather than a GWAS.
  6. Comparison with Arabidopsis. Compare with the author's Arabidopsis spaceflight analysis, which is in preparation.

Withdrawn on 14 October 2026

These results appeared on this site and in the draft manuscript. None came from an analysis, so they were removed:

  • The 15-accession gravitropic curvature survey and its ANOVA, and the 46-accession correlation figure: the values were generated from seeded random numbers.
  • The GWAS Manhattan and QQ plots: random p-values with named genes typed in as peaks; no genotype data existed.
  • Gene-level spaceflight fold changes, adjusted p-values, DEG counts and the volcano and enrichment panels for OSD-375: typed-in values; no differential-expression analysis was run.
  • The cross-species meta-analysis with Arabidopsis (odds ratio 13.70, p = 0.0446; and odds ratio 14.16, p = 3.54 × 10⁻¹²): computed from hand-entered flags.
  • The cereal synteny map and table, the promoter-motif enrichment and the alternative-splicing tables: typed-in values.
  • The mechanistic model's spaceflight panel, which listed up- and down-regulated genes taken from the typed-in values.
  • The Brachypodium gene IDs, chromosome positions and rice/wheat orthologs of the candidate genes: none of the IDs could be confirmed as an ortholog of its Arabidopsis anchor.
